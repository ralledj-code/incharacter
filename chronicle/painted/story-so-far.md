# The Story So Far (draft 1)

Lucien Vale, by Thorman's fire in the Down Deep, the night of episode 13. Arthas and Cedric are there, half asleep, half listening.
Each paragraph is one "beat" (2-4 paintings). [Brackets] = which level the pictures come from.

---

**1. The fire** [13]
Pull up a stone. Closer. The fire's the only honest thing down here. You want to know how three brothers who never chose each other ended up this far under Alaron? Then listen. And I'm telling it, so I'm the handsome one. Arthas, don't.

**2. Three strangers** [1]
It started in a harbour, with dice and a debt. I was a bard with a price on my name, buying herbs from a smuggler called Silas. Then a warehouse door opened, and two men stood in it. One in black plate who never laughs. One with eyes like a frozen lake. Arthas and Cedric. Same mother as me, it turned out. Different fathers. We had that in common, and nothing else.

**3. Silas turns** [1, 2]
That night Silas doubled over and came up a wolf. And far off in the pines, two green eyes were watching. Severin. Remember her, because she never forgot us. She'd walked through the next town with free potions for the sick. Mercy in a bottle. Silas's wife drank it. His neighbours drank it. Her teeth found my neck. And that night, my dagger started to hum.

**4. The children** [2]
We found Silas's children chained to their beds behind a door of runes. They hadn't turned. I begged for time. Cedric didn't give it, and Arthas didn't wait. We carried them to the church in blankets. I've never quite forgiven either of them. They've never asked me to.

**5. The secret I drank** [3]
The druids' moonwater heals the curse. We had one vial. Kids, Silas, or me. I drank it in a basement and filled it back up with water. That's the first lie in this story. It isn't the last. Brothers, you can stop pretending to be asleep.

**6. The wells** [3, 4]
Under Alaron, the moonwells hold something down. A god of beasts, chained by the moon herself. Someone has been poisoning the wells with black goo. We cleansed the first one in a dead swamp, between two brick arches. A monster rose out of the goo with a man melted into its belly. Then something swallowed me whole. A jelly. My brothers cut me out. They mention it often.

**7. The Pig** [4, 5, 6]
Behind the poison was the Pig. A butcher three metres tall who grows stronger with every death. We chased him into a mountain, into a slaughterhouse with a lake of blood. I threw a fireball through a lit window to burn him out. There were prisoners behind that window. I didn't know. That fire changed me. Arthas held his breath under that blood until the Pig went still. Then the black water came out of the rock, and the last prisoner opened her eyes. Severin. She offered to free the god in the wells. I poured her elixir on the floor.

**8. Their father** [6]
Up top waited High Inquisitor Valerys. Arthas's father. Cedric's father. Not mine, thank the gods. A hard man with something red-eyed under the grate in his floor. Hold on to that.

**9. Cobh** [7, 8]
He sent us to Cobh, to a dry moonwell. A druid called Neris met us there. He asked for our blood to wake the well. Arthas gave it. One chain snapped, and in the black water below I saw a face. Horns. Eyes like coals. Baphomet. Neris had a curse of his own, so he stole my dagger, drove it into his own chest, and walked out a man again. That's when I learned what the blade can do. It eats curses.

**10. The happy part** [7, 8]
I should tell you about the happy part. I caught lightning in a bottle so a lovesick professor could marry my ex. Cedric killed a dragon alone and carried its head home. Cobh threw a feast. I woke in a cell. Arthas woke in lipstick. Then the city burned.

**11. Saving Cobh** [9]
The Scarlet Wizards came with orcs and a troll the size of a house. I pulled Caroline clear, Arthas took the troll's head, and Cedric took an arrow in the knee so the rest of us could live. Their commander walked away saying, "We got what we came for." Behind the walls, a circle of red runes was still glowing.

**12. Mother** [10]
And then, in Mara's basement, our mother. Alive. Inside the Scarlet Wizards, slowing the poison from within. She told me never to seek my father, and never to listen to his melody. An arrow called her a traitor and she was gone again. I put on another man's face, walked into Ahm's halls on Happiness Street, and burned the place down. Under a bloody altar we found the truth. The merchants hold the king's son. And I came out with gills, and eyes that own the dark. Useful down here, as it turns out.

**13. The hunger** [11]
Valerys gave me a title, Clerical User of Nefarious Tools. Read the first letters. He did. Under the Ahm safehouse we found Silas again, more wolf than man. And Severin spoke through my dagger, the same voice from the pines. "Give in. Just this once." I did. It drank Silas's curse, then mine. Silas walked out human. I walked out stronger, and I liked it. I still do. That's the part I don't tell Mara.

**14. Duncastle** [12]
Mara. I gave her my last two thousand gold and told her to run. Then Duncastle fell. Mother had warned them days before and they laughed her off. Arthas broke a crystal and called his father for help. His father came. As an Oathbreaker. He burned through friend and foe alike, and then a serpent rose out of the courtyard and swallowed him whole.

**15. Down** [13]
So I stood on a crate and said I'd never meant to be here, but I care. We came down the cellar stair after the worm. We lost five good men to land sharks. Kalen stole my kill. Arthas missed, twice. His sword has never missed before. Maybe it knows what he isn't telling us. And tonight a voice slid into Cedric's head, offering us his father for one glowing spore.

**16. The fire, again** [13]
So that's us. A sorcerer with a hungry knife. A paladin whose sword has stopped believing him. A monk with a monster in his head. Same mother. Three fathers. One of them is down here somewhere, wearing a crown of black fire. The other two, nobody's told us about yet. Somewhere a melody is waiting for me, and I'm told not to listen. I've always been bad at that.
Now let the fire burn down, brothers. Tomorrow we go deeper.
