# The Story So Far (draft 6: a conversation)

Thorman's camp in the Down Deep, the night of episode 13. Lucien has pulled the drow woman away from the others to a small fire of his own
and is telling her his story to impress her. He flirts and jokes, but he has seen some things and it shows: tired, raspy, honest when it hurts. Never smug. She starts cold and dry, hard to impress; every time he stops performing and gets honest (Severin, the window, his mother) she warms a little more, until she is the one asking questions. Sweet voice, but a drow: the sweetness has an edge.
Canon from Rasmus's dossier (lucien_vale_dossier_v2): he came to Alaron hunting Severin Draik, his first love, who used him as the vessel in an infernal ritual; he should have died, it broke his magic into wild magic; the dagger she used is his focus and it whispers when he is sober; he was a smuggler of the good stuff (never anything that kills); he plays the flute; elves are frowned on in Alaron; nobody told him his mother was alive, he learned it from his brothers' letter.
Story first, fights in passing. Lines that are not from the sessions (her reactions, his flirting) are invented filler.
LUCIEN / DROW = who speaks. [Brackets] = which level the pictures come from. {Braces} = sound. The fire crackles the whole way through.

---

**1. The fire** [13]
DROW: You've been staring at me since you walked into this camp.
LUCIEN: I've been admiring the cave. You just happen to be standing in front of it.
DROW: It's a damp cave.
LUCIEN: It is a very damp cave. And you're wondering what someone this good-looking is doing in it. Sit. It's a good story. I'm in it.
DROW: Of course you are.

**2. Why I came** [1]
LUCIEN: I came to Alaron for one woman. Severin.
DROW: Of course you did.
LUCIEN: Not like that. She was the first person I ever really loved. Trusted her, too. And one night she put this knife in my chest in the middle of a ritual. I was supposed to die. {the fire crackles} I didn't. Whatever she pushed through me broke my magic. It's been wild ever since. Then I heard she was on this island.
DROW: So you came to kill her.
LUCIEN: I came to ask her if she meant it.
DROW: {a pause} ...Hm.

**3. The brothers** [1]
LUCIEN: It started, as all my best stories do, in a tavern. I played the flute for my supper and bought some herbs from a smuggler called Silas. I used to move the good stuff myself, once. Never anything that kills.
DROW: How noble.
LUCIEN: I have standards. Then a warehouse door opened, and in walked the two most serious men on Alaron. Arthas, in black plate, who has never once laughed at my jokes.
DROW: I'm starting to like him.
LUCIEN: And Cedric, who looks at you like he's already decided where to hit. My brothers. Same mother, different fathers. They'd had a letter: our mother was alive, somewhere on this island. Nobody wrote to me. I found out from them.
DROW: {quieter} That must have stung.
LUCIEN: Like a wasp in a boot. I smiled through it.

**4. The curse** [1, 2]
LUCIEN: That same night, Silas turned into a wolf. Rude. And out in the pines, two green eyes were watching us. Her. Severin. A pale woman with silver hair, whose potions Silas had been handing out to the sick, for free.
DROW: Nothing is free.
LUCIEN: Nothing is free. Silas's whole family turned. His wife bit me on the neck. So now I had a curse on top of her knife. It whispers to me, you know. Mostly when I'm sober.
DROW: Is it whispering now?
LUCIEN: ...I've had a drink. We're fine.

**5. My little secret** [3]
LUCIEN: The druids have this water from the moonwells. It heals curses. We had exactly one vial. The children needed it, Silas needed it, I needed it. So I drank it. In a basement. Alone. Then I filled it up with water and put it back.
DROW: That's terrible.
LUCIEN: It was tactical. And here's the funny part. It didn't even work.
DROW: Good.
LUCIEN: I'll come back to that.

**6. The wells** [3, 4]
LUCIEN: Those moonwells are prison locks. Under this island the moon goddess chained the Lord of Beasts. Baphomet. And someone is pouring black goo into the wells to let him out.
DROW: Everyone down here knows that name. Nobody says it out loud.
LUCIEN: I say it all the time. Baphomet. Baphomet. See? Nothing. We cleaned one of the wells. I was briefly eaten by a jelly.
DROW: Eaten.
LUCIEN: Briefly. Moving on.

**7. The Pig** [4, 5, 6]
LUCIEN: The someone was the Pig. A butcher the size of a barn, sending barrels of black blood across the island, town by town. We found him deep in a mountain. And I threw a fireball through a lit window to smoke him out. {the fire fades away} There were people behind that window. I didn't know.
DROW: {silence, then the fire creeps back} ...You don't joke about that one.
DROW: Then don't.
LUCIEN: No. We finished him. The black blood still runs. But that window stays with me.

**8. Their father** [6]
LUCIEN: My brothers' father is the High Inquisitor of the Church. Valerys. Stern, cold, always one step ahead. Not my father, thank the gods. I was sure he was hiding something. I watched him for weeks. Turns out he wasn't. Not then, anyway. I notice things.
DROW: You notice things.
LUCIEN: I noticed you.
DROW: Keep talking, sorcerer.

**9. Cobh** [7, 8]
LUCIEN: He sent us to Cobh, to a moonwell that had dried up. A druid there asked for our blood to wake it, and Arthas, ever helpful, gave his. One of the great chains snapped, and in the black water I saw a face. Horns. Burning eyes. Looking right at us.
DROW: The one you say out loud.
LUCIEN: The very same. Then the druid stabbed himself with my dagger, and his wolf curse simply fell away.
DROW: Your knife eats curses.
LUCIEN: It does.
DROW: And what does it do to you?
LUCIEN: ...That's the question, isn't it.

**10. My talents** [7, 9]
LUCIEN: Cobh gave me some of my finest work. I caught lightning in a bottle {thunder} so a lovesick professor could finally marry my ex.
DROW: You have an ex in every town.
LUCIEN: Two in that one. Then a black dragon came down out of a red sky, and Cedric chased it alone and killed it by himself. He still brags about it to this day. Every. Single. Day. Cobh threw us a feast. I woke up in a cell. Arthas woke up in lipstick.
DROW: Were you involved?
LUCIEN: Prove it.

**11. Mother** [10]
LUCIEN: And then, in a basement, our mother. Alive. All those years inside the Scarlet Wizards, slowing their poison from within. The woman who left me, and never wrote. She told me never to look for my father, and if I ever hear his melody, not to listen.
DROW: And you sing.
LUCIEN: Melodies are my whole thing. Then an arrow called her a traitor, and she was gone again.
DROW: Did you ever find out why she left you?
LUCIEN: No. She's good at leaving. It runs in the family.
DROW: You're still here.
LUCIEN: ...So I am.

**12. The Prince** [10]
LUCIEN: So I put on another man's face, walked into the Ahm merchants' headquarters, and politely burned it down. Under a bloody altar we found the truth: they hold the king's son, Prince Oswald. And I came out of there with gills.
DROW: Gills.
LUCIEN: Very useful down here. I can see in the dark now, too. You have lovely eyes, by the way.
DROW: Everyone down here can see in the dark.
LUCIEN: Then everyone down here knows you have lovely eyes.

**13. My title** [11]
LUCIEN: Then the Church gave me a title. Officially. I am a Clerical User of Nefarious Tools.
DROW: That spells...
LUCIEN: It does. I had it engraved. Then, under a safehouse, we found Silas again, more wolf than man. Her knife spoke to me, in her voice. Severin's voice. "Give in. Just this once." So I did. It drank the curse out of Silas, and then out of me. Remember the moonwater? Didn't work. The knife did.
DROW: And how did that feel?
LUCIEN: Honestly? I quite liked it.
DROW: That should worry you.
LUCIEN: It does. When I'm sober. So I try not to be.

**14. Duncastle** [12]
LUCIEN: Mara, my oldest friend, I sent away with my last gold. Then Duncastle fell. Mother had warned them days before, and they laughed at her. Arthas called on his father for help, and his father came. But not as the man they knew. An Oathbreaker, burning through friend and enemy alike. And then a giant serpent rose out of the ground and swallowed him whole. {a deep rumble}
DROW: ...The worm.
LUCIEN: The worm.

**15. Why I'm here** [13]
LUCIEN: So I climbed onto a crate and gave the speech of my life, and people followed me down a cellar stair, into the dark, after it. Which brings us here. Arthas's sword keeps missing, and it never misses. And tonight something in these caves spoke into Cedric's head and offered to give them their father back.
DROW: Down here, nothing is offered. Everything is bought.
LUCIEN: Then we'll find out the price.

**16. Tonight** [13]
DROW: So. A woman you came here to find. A hungry knife that used to be hers. A mother in the shadows. A melody you mustn't hear. A prince to find. And a god of beasts waking up under your feet.
LUCIEN: When you put it like that, it sounds bad.
DROW: It sounds like you might not live very long. {softer} I'd rather you did.
LUCIEN: Which is exactly why I shouldn't waste tonight.
DROW: {a soft laugh} That was terrible.
LUCIEN: But it's working.
DROW: ...It's working.
