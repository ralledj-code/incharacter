# The Story So Far (draft 2)

Lucien Vale, by Thorman's fire in the Down Deep, the night of episode 13. Proud of himself, not taking any of it very seriously,
Arthas and Cedric half asleep beside him. Story first, fights only in passing. The question it answers: why am I sitting in this damp cave?
Each paragraph is one "beat" (2-4 paintings). [Brackets] = which level the pictures come from. ~930 words.

---

**1. The damp cave** [13]
You're wondering why someone this good-looking is sitting in a damp cave at the bottom of the world. Fair question. Pull up a rock, the fire's the only warm thing down here. Well, almost the only one. I'll get to that.

**2. How it started** [1]
It started, as all my best stories do, in a tavern. I was a bard with a small price on my head and a smuggler called Silas who sold me herbs. Then a warehouse door opened and in walked the two most serious men on Alaron. Arthas, in black plate, who has never once laughed at my jokes. And Cedric, who looks at you like he's already decided where to hit. My brothers, it turned out. Same mother. Different fathers. Mine was clearly the handsome one.

**3. The curse** [1, 2]
That same night, Silas turned into a wolf. Rude. And out in the pines, two green eyes were watching us. Severin. A pale woman with silver hair who had walked through the next town handing out free medicine to the sick. Nothing in this world is free. Silas's whole family turned. His wife bit me on the neck. So now I had a curse, and my dagger started humming whenever I was nervous. Which was often.

**4. My little secret** [3]
Now, the druids have this water from the moonwells. It heals. We had exactly one vial, and the children needed it, and Silas needed it, and so did I. I drank it. In a basement. Alone. Then I topped it up with water and put it back. A tactical decision. And here's the funny part: it didn't even work. The curse stayed. I'll come back to that.

**5. The wells** [3, 4]
Those moonwells, it turns out, are prison locks. Under this island, the moon goddess chained something called the Lord of Beasts. Baphomet. And someone is pouring black goo into the wells to set him loose. We cleaned one. It went fine, mostly. I was briefly eaten by a jelly. Moving on.

**6. Their father** [6]
Up at the church, my brothers' father waits. High Inquisitor Valerys. Stern, cold, always one step ahead. Not my father, thank the gods. When we came to him with blood on our boots, he barely looked up. And there was something with red eyes under the grate in his floor. I noticed. Nobody else did. I notice things.

**7. Cobh** [7, 8]
He sent us to Cobh, to a moonwell that had dried up. A druid named Neris met us there and asked for our blood to wake it. Arthas, ever helpful, gave his. One of the great chains snapped, and in the black water I saw a face. Horns. Burning eyes. Baphomet, looking right at us. Then Neris grabbed my dagger, stabbed himself with it, and his wolf curse simply fell away. So the knife eats curses. Interesting. Filed that away.

**8. My talents** [7, 9]
Cobh also gave me some of my finest work. I caught lightning in a bottle so a lovesick professor could finally marry my ex, Caroline. You're welcome, Caroline. I rescued another old flame from her horrible father. Cobh threw us a feast. I woke up in a cell. Arthas woke up in lipstick. Some say I was involved. I say prove it.

**9. Mother** [10]
And then, in Mara's basement, our mother. Alive. All these years, living inside the Scarlet Wizards, slowing their poison from within. She gave me one piece of advice: never look for your father, and if you ever hear his melody, don't listen. I'm a bard. Melodies are my whole thing. Then an arrow called her a traitor, and she was gone again.

**10. The Prince** [10]
So I put on another man's face, walked us into the Ahm merchants' headquarters, and politely burned it down. Under a bloody altar we found the truth: the merchants hold the king's son, Prince Oswald, and that's why the king does nothing while they swallow the island. I also came out of there with gills. Long story. Very useful down here.

**11. My title** [11]
Then the Church gave me a title. Me. Officially. I am a Clerical User of Nefarious Tools. Say the first letters out loud. Go on. I had it engraved. And under the merchants' safehouse we found Silas again, more wolf than man. My dagger hummed, and a voice I knew spoke through it. Severin. "Give in. Just this once." So I did. It drank the curse right out of Silas, and then right out of me. Remember the moonwater? Didn't work. The knife did. Silas walked out a man. I walked out stronger. And honestly? I quite liked it.

**12. Duncastle** [12]
Mara, my oldest friend, I sent away with my last gold. Then we went to Duncastle, the Church's fortress, and it fell. Mother had warned them days before. They laughed at her. Arthas called on his father for help, and his father came. But not as the man they knew. An Oathbreaker, burning through friend and enemy alike. And then a giant serpent rose out of the ground and swallowed him whole.

**13. Why I'm here** [13]
So I climbed onto a crate and gave the speech of my life. "I was never meant to be here, but I care." People followed. Into the cellar, down a stair, into the dark, after the worm. Which brings us here. Arthas's sword keeps missing, and it never misses. Something in this cave spoke into Cedric's head tonight and offered to give us their father back. For a price.

**14. The fire** [13]
So that's why I'm in a damp cave. Two brothers whose father became a monster. One knife that's getting hungry. A mother in the shadows. A melody I'm not allowed to listen to. A prince to find. And somewhere down here, the thing that started all of it is waking up.
But tonight? Tonight the fire's warm. And so is she. Goodnight, brothers. Try not to snore.

## Threads it ties together
- **The curse:** the bite (3), the moonwater that didn't work (4), "I'll come back to that" paid off by the dagger (11).
- **The dagger:** humming when nervous (3), Neris shows what it does (7), Severin's voice and the hunger (11), "one knife that's getting hungry" (14).
- **Severin:** green eyes in the pines and free medicine (3), the voice in the dagger (11).
- **Mother:** same mother (2), the warning about the melody (9), laughed off at Duncastle (12), "a melody I'm not allowed to listen to" (14).
- **Valerys:** stern father (6), red eyes under the grate (6), the Oathbreaker (12), the offer (13).
- **Baphomet:** the wells as prison locks (5), the face in the water (7), "the thing that started all of it is waking up" (14).
- **The warm one:** "almost the only one, I'll get to that" (1), paid off in the last lines (14).
- Fights are one line each: the jelly (5), the Ahm fire (10), the Duncastle siege (12). The Pig, the dragon, Cobh's siege and the bulettes are left out on purpose.
