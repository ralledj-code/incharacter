Dolkan (hastig, hæs og sammensvoren):
Hold the line, boys! My magic sees the invisible chain wrapped around your father’s throat! I know him—I stood beneath the towering arches of Kair Kal-ih-deer before he cast me into the dark! Look through my memory! Rain lashes the stained glass of Tyr’s sanctum. Your mother has vanished into the dark, leaving no trace behind... and upon the cold stone lie two small boys—you! Pale as chalk, drowning in sweat, with a black, unnatural poison creeping through your veins toward your hearts! No healer in the realm could break it. And there, stripped of his armor and pride, kneels a younger Valerious—terrified of losing the only pieces of her he has left!
​Valerious (hæs, knækket og desperat):
Their mother is gone, Tyr... do not take them from me too! Take me instead! Strip my title, take my honor, take every breath left in my lungs! Just burn this poison from my boys... let them live!
​Dolkan (dæmpet og anspændt):
The torches freeze into blinding white fire without heat. Shadows stretch like iron bars across the stone. And the one-handed god answers—not like thunder, no, like a thousand blades drawn at once:
​Týr (dyb, tordnende og kold):
The Law demands balance, Valerious. No life is given without a price. These boys carry blood that the darkness already hunts. If I am to pull them from the grave, you must become the wall that keeps the shadow out.
​Valerious (hæs og forpustet):
Name the price. Anything!
​Týr (dyb, tordnende og absolut):
Your sword and your oath belong to Caer Callidyrr. You will never cross this city’s gates again—neither to hunt their mother, nor to ride to their aid. You will be the city’s shield for as long as your heart beats. The day you place your own blood above your post and step beyond these walls, the contract is shattered—and the very oath you swore will consume you.
​Dolkan (tung og intens):
See the agony in his eyes! In that single heartbeat, he realizes the terrible choice: to keep you breathing, he must abandon the search for your mother forever! He knows he must send little Cedric away to the monastery to hide your blood from the cult... and he must hold Arthas at arm's length, cold and distant, terrified that if he ever lets himself love you openly, he will grow weak enough to break his vow and doom you all! Still, he doesn't flinch. He lays his sword at the foot of the altar and opens his arms to Tyr’s blinding judgment—letting the god's searing golden light wash over you, burning the poison from your veins while forging his own soul to the city's stone!
​Valerious (anstrengt og stille beslutsom):
Then let it be the law. Let my life be their chain.
​Dolkan (intens og råbende over kampen):
Tyr never betrayed him! Your father bought your lives with his own—and he shattered that chain in Doncastle just to save you! Stop praying to a god who has already collected his due! His only chance of survival is to deny his oath to Tyr! Now fight!




​Setting: Det dybeste, skyggefulde kammer under Týrs katedral i Caer Callidyrr. Høje blyindfattede ruder piskes af natte-regn. En kolossal statue af den enarmede gud Týr tårner sig op med sværd og vægtskål.
​Karakterer i billedet: En tyve år yngre Valerious (kulsort hår, pladerustning smidt til side) knæler på det kolde stengulv. Ved siden af ham på uldtæpper ligger to små drenge (5–6 år gamle, Arthas og Cedric), blege og feberramte med mørke, pulserende årer på halsen. Dolkan ses ikke fysisk i katedralen, men hans stemme skærer gennem luften som en magisk projektion.
