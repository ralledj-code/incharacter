# Alaron Noire: intro ("Calm Night"), long version

Plays after **New case** and before the player wakes at the desk in Maren's room. Length about **2:40**. 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 or 30 fps.

## The idea in one line

Why does a tired former fire-warden spend her nights reading merchants' ledgers? Because a ledger killed her husband, and the city let it.

## What the intro has to do

1. **Make us care about Maren.** Show her husband Aurel alive and laughing before we lose him.
2. **Plant her two skills.**
   - She reads how a fire burned.
   - She reads numbers.

   Both matter in the game.
3. **Show the city.**
   - The Ahm own half of it.
   - The Church of Tyr watches it.
   - Magic is done quietly or not at all.
   - Elves are not welcome.
4. **Introduce the tools and the crew.**
   - The Quiet Office and Director Cross.
   - The brass lantern, which shows traces of magic.
   - Brann and Pell.
5. **Hand over to the game.** End in the same attic where the player wakes.

## Rules for the intro

- **Narrator.** Maren narrates in her own voice: dry, tired, decent. She never sounds sorry for herself. A little dry humour is fine, the way tired professionals are funny.
- **The three strangers.**
  - Never show the elf, the giant in plate or the monk.
  - Don't hint at them. The game's rule is traces before faces.
- **Nothing gory.** The fire is seen from outside. There are no bodies.
- **The look.**
  - The same painted style as the game: painterly fantasy and noir.
  - Fog, rain, lanterns, wet cobbles.
  - The palette is warm candle and fire light against cold blue-grey.
  - The `reference/place_*.jpg` images are the game's own paintings. Match those.
- **Maren now.**
  - She is in her forties, with shoulder-length dark brown hair.
  - She wears a brown leather coat with a teal lining and a high collar. Her face is tired but sharp.
  - `reference/maren_portrait.png` shows exactly this.
- **Maren then (shots 2–8).**
  - She is about ten years younger, with her hair tied back.
  - At work she is a fire-warden. She wears a soot-streaked leather helmet and a heavy canvas coat, and carries a fire axe.
  - At home she wears simple wool clothes.
- **Aurel.**
  - Aurel was her husband, in his thirties. He has warm brown curly hair and is always about to laugh. He hated sitting still.
  - There is no reference image for him. Any face that matches this description is fine, as long as it stays the same in every shot.
- **The warehouse owner.** A plump, prosperous man in rich clothes. We never learn his name.
- **Aldraik.** The Ahm merchant king appears only as a painted portrait or a statue, never moving. Use `reference/aldraik_portrait.png`.
- **Director Cross.**
  - She is a stern woman in her fifties, with long silver hair and a black high-collared coat. Use `reference/director_cross_portrait.png`.
- **Brann.**
  - He is a bald old watchman in his sixties, with a big moustache, a dark greatcoat and a clay pipe. Use `reference/brann_portrait.png`.
- **Pell.**
  - He is a young clerk of about twenty-three. He has tousled brown hair and round spectacles, and wears a burgundy waistcoat. He is always drawing.

**Delivery.**
- Keep it low and unhurried, and pause where the commas and full stops are.
- **Act 1:** almost fond.
- **Act 2:** flat and controlled. The "Aurel was inside" line is the quietest in the film, but it doesn't break.
- **Act 4 onwards:** back to dry, with a little humour.
- **The last line:** almost a smile.
