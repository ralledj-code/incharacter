WHAT CHANGED FROM v1 (Rasmus: too few pictures, too slow, too many effects, no subtitles; it has to feel Chronicle)
- 39 paintings instead of 14 (about 4.2 s each, quick cuts only at the fire). 10 are the kit's storyboard frames,
  29 are new paintings of the script's own beats, from the game's cast portraits (Maren, Cross, Brann, Pell, Aldraik),
  the intro portraits (the mentor, the magistrate) and the storyboard's own Aurel, young Maren and owner, so faces match.
- The Chronicle player's motion: camera moves scaled to each shot, haze, embers/rain/dust, flicker, fades and slow
  dissolves, a white flash as the fire hits.
- Sound: Maren's 14 recorded lines exactly as recorded; an invented, very low underscore (Lyria: warm guitar and cello
  in Act 1, dark low strings from the fire on), ducked under her voice about 17-20 dB down; only 9 quiet effects, each on
  its cut (crackle, the fire, rain and a bell, the stamp, rain, the lantern's hum, gulls, the temple bell, the attic rain).
  No ambience bed under every shot any more.
- Subtitles: Maren's lines in 39 phrases, aligned to the recording.
- The script's own beats are now shown: the painter's room and Maren laughing in the doorway, the old fire-warden's
  finger on the char pattern, the coat round her shoulders, the stamp, the bored magistrate, the carriage to the villa,
  Maren outside the gate, the owner laughing at his party, the door with no sign, Maren and Cross across the desk, the lantern
  slid across, the shimmer on the doorframe, banners, coins into a crimson glove, Aldraik ONLY as a gilded portrait,
  a hooded figure with a pointed ear (anonymous, never one of the three strangers), Brann with his pipe, Pell's sketch
  of Maren, Maren asleep in the attic with Aurel's small portrait, ending on the portrait.
