# Alaron Chronicle

Painted music videos for Rasmus's D&D campaign (island of Alaron): one Suno song per session, sung by Lucien Vale (half-elf wild magic sorcerer, Rasmus's character), shown as a sequence of paintings synced to the song. Lyrics come from InCharacter session logs.

Status (2026-11-21): levels 0-14 are painted and live, plus four hidden family songs (?song=tine-max, ?song=nutella, ?song=krokodille, ?song=funky, all on the family page /chronicle/familie or ?familie). **The 3D tavern, songbook and 3D rides were removed on 2026-11-21** (Rasmus: "remove the old Inn entirely"); they are in git history before that date if ever needed. Checked on a Galaxy Fold 7 by Rasmus; not yet on an iPhone or the Legion Go.

## Where it lives
Inside the InCharacter repo (ralledj-code/incharacter). Source: `chronicle/`. Build output: `public/chronicle/index.html` (committed) next to `public/chronicle/levels/` (songs + web paintings). Served at `/chronicle` and `/chronicle/familie` (rewrites in next.config.mjs). Public, no login (middleware skips it).

## Commands
- In `chronicle/`: `npm i && npm run build` -> ../public/chronicle/index.html. **Rebuild and commit the output after every src change.** `npm run dev` builds and serves public/chronicle standalone. `npm test` = node smoke test: every playable level's line/voice counts, audio file, shot order and that every painting exists (no rendering).
- Dev hooks: `window.__alaron.dev.hold = { t, time }` freezes the player on a song time; `dev.shot(state)` renders one frame; `__alaron.seek(t)`; `__alaron.play(id)`. `tools/pshot.cjs` uses them for stills and mp4s.
- Three.js r128 from cdnjs (global UMD), only for the painted player. Pin exact version.

## Architecture (src/, IIFEs sharing `window.__cw`; build order in scripts/build.mjs, levels/*.js auto-included)
- w1_core: helpers (clamp, lerp, ...) and level 8's song data (CUES, LINE_TIMES). w1b_base: `C.BASE()` (asset base, also for /chronicle/familie).
- w5_painted: the painted player (`C.createPainted`): shots, transitions, pan/zoom, particles, post pass.
- levels/LNN-<id>.js: one module per song (audio, cues, lineTimes, beats, titles, `hidden`/`feature` flags). painted/<NN-id>.json: the storyboards (built into C.boards).
- s0_stories: the PLAYLIST; a level plays when it has audio and a live board, otherwise Coming soon.
- ui.js: song list (incl. family page and solo links), loading, audio, the player controls (standard video layout: back + title on top; seek bar, play/pause, -10/+10 s, mute, time, captions, settings (brightness, calm camera, lyric sync), landscape for rotation-locked phones, fullscreen; auto-hide after 3 s; mouse click = play/pause, tap = show/hide controls; keys Space/K, arrows, J/L, M, F, C, 0-9, Esc), lyrics with per-voice colour, end screen, wake lock + media session.
- New levels: **LEVELS.md** + `.claude/skills/chronicle-level`. Tools: `tools/pshot.cjs`, `tools/sheet.cjs`.

## Painted levels (the format now; runbook for the next one)
Look: Warcraft-style painted shorts (Burdens of Shaohao / Lords of War): one painting per shot, cut-out depth layers, slow drift, fog, particles, ink/fade/flash transitions, synced to the song. Rasmus loves it; ~45 paintings per song "works very well".
- **Files.** Storyboard `painted/<NN-id>.json` = `live`, `cover` (shot id for the song card), `style` (shared prompt, keep it as in 01), `cast` (character descriptions, go into every prompt the character is in), `shots` [{ id, t, in: cut|fade|ink|black|flash, who: [cast ids], fx: embers|sparks|dust|ash|snow|fireflies|fog|smoke|fire (flame glow licking up from the bottom), flicker, sway, shake, grade: red|cold|green, move {from,to: [x, y, push]}, scene, img: '<other shot id>' reuses that painting }]. Cast sheets `painted/<NN-id>/cast/*.jpg` (committed; copy existing ones for recurring characters). Raw art `painted/<NN-id>/raw/` (gitignored). Web art `public/chronicle/levels/<NN-id>/<shot>.webp + _d.png`.
- **Steps** (all in chronicle/):
  1. Read the level's song data (cues, LINE_TIMES, beats in `src/levels/` or `w1_core`) and scene comments, write back the story as the code tells it, and **ask Rasmus every question that changes a painting** before spending anything. The code and lyrics are often not what happened: level 8's "well" is a lake, Baphomet never rises (only his face in the black water), the morning after is Arthas in makeup / Cedric in furs / Lucien in jail, the song ends mid-fight; level 6: no wolf from the well, Stagford is far away, Severin speaks through the prisoner, Valerys is Arthas and Cedric's father. Unknowns (Ragnar) are left out.
  2. Storyboard: copy `painted/make/06.py`, write the shot list (one shot per lyric line or two; instrumental gaps get 1 shot per ~8 s; repeated choruses reuse paintings via `img`). It snaps hard cuts to the measured hits/bars/beats. Target ~40-45 paintings for a 5-6 min song.
  **Modus operandi (since 2026-10-27): Flash 3.1 + batch.** `paint.mjs` defaults to `gemini-3.1-flash-image` in batch mode: `cast`/`shots` submit a job, `node scripts/paint.mjs <NN-id> collect --wait 60` saves the results (the 2-image test took ~2 min; Google allows up to 24 h; rerun collect until done). `--now` paints immediately at full price (single repaints), `--model gemini-3-pro-image` for Pro. Flash vs Pro on the same prompts: characters as consistent, still bakes black bars sometimes (keep crop-bars), a little plainer compositions (straight lineups, wider than asked) - write the framing explicitly ("tight close-up of the face", "three-quarter view, staggered").
  3. `node scripts/paint.mjs <NN-id> cast <new ids> --max N` (then collect), look at every sheet (Gemini sometimes paints a scene instead of a sheet, or mixes two people: repaint once with a sharper description). Trim baked bars off sheets.
  4. Paint 2-3 test shots of the hardest content (`--now` for speed), look, then `... shots --max <paintings+4>` for the rest as one batch, and collect.
  5. Contact sheet of every painting; repaint only clear failures. Typical: **Lucien off-model in group shots** (dark hair, no coat: name his blond hair and ears in the scene text), extra or invented people (a shot with people but no `who` gets random strangers: always set `who`), creatures leaving where the story says they stay.
  6. `python scripts/crop-bars.py <NN-id>` (Gemini paints letterbox bars into ~25% of shots despite the prompt), then `python scripts/layers.py <NN-id> --model <depth_anything_v2_vits.onnx>` (curl it from github.com/fabio-sim/Depth-Anything-ONNX/releases/download/v2.0.0/depth_anything_v2_vits.onnx into the scratchpad). Set `live: true`, `npm run build && npm test`, play it headless from `?song=<id>` and look at frames at key lyric times.
